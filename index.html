A Beginner Guide for Volleyball 🏐



Rules

  • Maximum of 3 hits only.
  • You need to hit or over the ball within the court. If it is on the line, that counts as in.
  • One person = one touch.

    • Tips on how to play volleyball

  • Bend your knees for better control when receiving a ball.
  • When the ball is near, shuffle back and receive normally.
  • When diving, make sure chest first then just let yourself slide.
  • When serving, make sure you have proper contact on the ball and make sure the ball is high enough for you to hit it.
